# Data, forecast and battery validation card

The completed study tests modelled ageing at matched realized reliability. It does not validate physical lifetime extension or IEEE novelty. See [results](../MPC_RESEARCH.md) and [machine-readable evidence](evidence/age_mpc_v2/README.md).

| Object | Completed evidence | Remaining limitation |

| Indian demand | Primary workbook SHA-256, CC BY 4.0 registry metadata, MW header, continuous 12,984 hourly records, exact agreement with pinned mirror | Scaled national demand is not site demand; 2022–2023 remain a pinned mirror rather than independently reaggregated raw records |
| Timebase | Strict UTC weather; explicit UTC/IST half-hour overlap averaging; primary solar peak at local noon corroborates IST interpretation | Primary timestamps are naive and timezone is not explicitly declared in registry; processed-series causality is not field data-availability validation |

## Interpretation and next confirmatory study

The 95% deficit envelope achieves 91.26% aggregate holdout coverage and 70.39% in February. Do not describe this as a fully calibrated uncertainty model. Fixed marginal diagnostic intervals achieve about 75.11% coverage against a nominal 80% on future leads; learned pooled future MAE is 5.299 kW versus weekly persistence 4.591 kW. Future work should develop drift-sensitive seasonal calibration on new development data, diagnose coverage for sustained renewable deficits, and freeze another genuinely untouched season/site before a confirmatory run.

A low-SOC charging policy has a clear mechanism for reducing a calendar-exposure surrogate, but matching that surrogate to the offline model is only an internal check. Released ranking diagnostics quantify that relationship without fitting coefficients to test results. The real validation requirement is independent shallow-cycle LFP capacity-loss data (or a published model validated over the actual operating envelope), including relevant temperature, SOC and C-rate ranges. No available measurements here satisfy it.

For an IEEE submission, perform a targeted related-work review before asserting novelty, justify the uncertainty/reserve formulation, obtain a real Indian site's hourly load and credible plant conversion, and evaluate multiple untouched seasons/sites with matched reliability. Use uncertainty over episodes and battery parameters in addition to within-episode block resampling. This completed experiment supplies transparent engineering evidence and an inconclusive superiority finding, not publication readiness.
